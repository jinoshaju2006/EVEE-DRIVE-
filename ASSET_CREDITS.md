- **Kenney Car Kit** — five GLB models used by EVEE DRIVE: `sedanSports.glb`, `race.glb`, `suv.glb`, `truck.glb`, and `van.glb`.
- Creator/source: Kenney, [official Car Kit page](https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit).

The GLB files were sourced from a public Godot sample repository that contains the Kenney Car Kit models; the original asset creator and licence remain Kenney / CC0. This project does not claim ownership of the models.

## Usage notes

- The models are used as in-game vehicle visuals, not redistributed as a standalone asset pack.
- Vehicle movement and collision remain controlled by EVEE DRIVE's own scripts.
- Before a public release, re-check the upstream licence and verify the final imported models in the Android export.
